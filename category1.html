Research Design

Composite Thermal Conductivity Experiment

  • Designed and executed an experiment to demonstrate the thermal conductivity differences between graded, uniform, and plain steel.
    • Thermal videos with time lapses were obtained to show different cooling/heating rates.
    • Thermoprobe sensor data was graphed and smoothed to observe cooling/heating and differences across the steel samples.
  • Simulated cooling/heating scenarios in Solidworks and designed experiments through purchasing, assembly and analysis of data.
  • Used Python to graph, smoothen and analyze data, including 3D data. Worked with thermal cameras and cooling apparatuses and hot plates. Devised mechanical fixture solutions for construction of experiments.
  • The data was presented at HI-AM 2026 as results in geometry driven additive manufacturing for functionally graded thermal properties.

Machine Customization and Development

Augmented SLA Printer

  • With an existing SLA Phrozen printer, customizations, such as machined parts, printed parts, and vacuum pumps were used to create an environment that allowed the printer to print within 10 microns of precision.
    • Machined aluminum along with resin printed parts were combined with a vacuum table to hold the printing plate.
  • Ideated and designed pieces in CAD, purchasing needed parts for the entire assembly, and operating the modified printer. Ran different trials with varying resin qualities to obtain desired thickness.
  • Used Solidworks to design and assemble the entire modified build plate. Printed varying designs with extensive work in the SLA slicing software Chitubox and documenting the process.
  • The desired thickness was achieved, although with a 20 micron precision. Clients were given a jumping off point with further designs for an updated assembly to achieve a more precise print.

Testing and Construction

Shock Absorber Simulator

  • Built an automotive strut and shock absorber simulator at Paragon Systems Testing for client purchase.
    • Simultaneously simulates action on two pairs of hinges by by pushing weighted and lifting said gate by pneumatics.
    • Uses proximity sensors to detect when gate is down or up and activates pistons accordingly.
  • Assembled the entire machine by following a Solidworks design and cutting bosch frames, bolting the parts together and wiring the sensors to the control panel.
  • Used bosch cutters, bandsaws, Solidworks, and troubleshooted proximity sensors.
  • The machine operated reliably and could perform 2000+ cycles in 85℃ and -30℃ continuously.

Testing and Validation

Hood Hinge Cycling Test

  • Built and ran tester to simulate opening and closing action for six pairs of car hood hinges simultaneously for Paragon Systems Testing.
    • Simultaneously simulates action on 6 pairs of hinges by by pushing and falling with pneumatic pistons.
    • Fixtures were attached to hinges that simulated overall mass, and center of mass.
    • Pistons were programmed and calibrated to push open for some time, close for some closing speed, and remain closed for some dwell time.
    • Test was ran for 15,000 cycles in 85℃ and also in-30℃ and also in 90% relative humidity.
    • Records and observations were taken during, for gaps and torques.
  • I assembled the entire machine and assisted in developing the design of a simultaneous six hinges tester with each piston pushing on a beam that propelled three hinges at a time.
  • Used bosch cutters, bandsaws, and setup thermal chambers.
  • The machine operated reliably and could perform successfully perform 15,000 and the samples were reported a success.
